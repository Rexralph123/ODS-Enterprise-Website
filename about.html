About O.D.S Enterprise

Trusted electronics retailer, built on quality and customer care.

Our Story

O.D.S Enterprise started with a simple mission: make quality electronics accessible, affordable, and easy to buy with confidence. What began as a small phone and gadget outlet has grown into a full online store trusted by thousands of customers across Nigeria for genuine products, fair prices, and dependable service.

What We Offer

Genuine Products

Every phone, laptop, and accessory we sell is 100% authentic and sourced from verified suppliers.

Fast Delivery

Nationwide shipping with 2-day delivery in major cities and real-time order tracking.

Dedicated Support

Our customer care team is available 24/7 to help with orders, returns, and product questions.

Our Journey

2019
O.D.S Enterprise founded as a small electronics retail shop in Lagos, focused on phones and accessories.
2021
Expanded product range to include laptops, wearables, and smart home gadgets. Opened a second location.
2023
Launched nationwide delivery and crossed 10,000 customers served.
2026
Brought the full O.D.S Enterprise experience online, with secure accounts, order tracking, and a wider catalog than ever.
